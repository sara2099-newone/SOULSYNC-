COMMON CHALLENGES

Common Student Challenges

College life brings exciting opportunities along with unique challenges. Understanding them is the first step toward maintaining good mental well-being.

Examinations can feel overwhelming, especially when expectations are high. Feeling nervous is normal, but effective preparation and healthy routines can reduce stress.

Quick Tips
  • Create a realistic study schedule.
  • Take regular short breaks.
  • Get enough sleep before exams.

Being away from family and familiar surroundings can be emotionally challenging, especially during your first few months of college.

Quick Tips
  • Stay connected with family.
  • Build new friendships.
  • Participate in campus activities.

Too much screen time can affect focus, sleep quality and emotional well-being.

Quick Tips
  • Reduce screen time before bed.
  • Take regular digital breaks.
  • Spend time outdoors.

Meeting new people or speaking in groups may feel difficult. Confidence grows through small positive experiences.

Quick Tips
  • Start with small conversations.
  • Join clubs and communities.
  • Celebrate small achievements.

Many students feel uncertain about internships, placements and future career opportunities.

Quick Tips
  • Develop new skills.
  • Talk to mentors.
  • Set realistic career goals.

Managing studies, extracurricular activities and personal life requires effective planning.

Quick Tips
  • Use a planner.
  • Prioritize important tasks.
  • Avoid procrastination.
MYTHS VS FACTS

Breaking Mental Health Myths

Let's separate common misconceptions from reality and promote a healthier understanding of mental well-being.

Myth

Only weak people seek counselling.

Fact

Seeking support is a sign of strength, courage and self-awareness.

Myth

Stress is always harmful.

Fact

Moderate stress can improve motivation and performance.

Myth

Mental health problems are uncommon among students.

Fact

Many students experience stress, anxiety and emotional challenges.

Myth

Talking about emotions makes things worse.

Fact

Sharing your feelings often helps reduce emotional burden.

SUPPORT COMPASS

How Are You Feeling Today?

Everyone experiences different emotions. Here are simple practical tips to help you take the next step toward better mental well-being.

Always Stressed

Helpful Tips
  • Break large tasks into smaller ones.
  • Take regular short breaks.
  • Practice deep breathing.

Can't Focus

Helpful Tips
  • Study in a quiet place.
  • Use the Pomodoro technique.
  • Remove phone distractions.

Overwhelmed

Helpful Tips
  • Focus on one task at a time.
  • Prioritize important work.
  • Ask for help when needed.

Feeling Lonely

Helpful Tips
  • Talk with a trusted friend.
  • Join clubs or activities.
  • Stay connected with family.

Feeling Anxious

Helpful Tips
  • Take slow deep breaths.
  • Write your thoughts.
  • Talk with someone you trust.

Sleep Problems

Helpful Tips
  • Sleep at the same time daily.
  • Avoid screens before bedtime.
  • Keep your room calm and dark.
